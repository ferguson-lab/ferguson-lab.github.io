EVENT LOG

    WHAT THE SPOT IS TELLING YOU

    1. 1 One unresolved spot. The gene is far below the diffraction limit, so the microscope sees a single PSF-sized spot of fixed width (FWHM ≈ 290 nm). Nothing inside it is resolved — only its two-channel brightness changes.
    2. 2 NIR counts TFs. Each promoter site holds one NIR-labelled TF. I_NIR = n_bound × brightness/dye, so the magenta channel steps up and down as TFs dock and leave. Initiation follows (n/sites)^nH.
    3. 3 Green counts RNA × GFP. RNAP elongates at 25 nt/s; each nascent RNA dwells 67 s (1675 nt). Its 24 MS2 loops emerge progressively and each stochastically binds MCP-GFP. I_green = Σ loops_bound × brightness/GFP — watch it ramp, plateau and drop as RNAs terminate.
    4. 4 FCCS reads causality. Cross-correlating NIR(t) with green(t+τ) peaks at a positive lag — the transcription delay between TF occupancy and labelled RNA. Raise ε crosstalk to see an instantaneous fake peak at τ→0.
    Magenta haze = fast-diffusing unbound TF pool (motion-blurred, out of focus). Green motes = released mRNPs leaving the volume. Ring colour = photons per orbit bin · dashed vector = tracking error · the orbit center steers to keep the spot locked through every intensity step.
